Put your PDF papers here. Subdirectories are supported.

Suggested structure:
    bdim/           - BDIM and BDIM2 papers (Weymouth & Yue, Maertens, etc.)
    ibm/            - Other immersed boundary methods
    multigrid/      - Multigrid and iterative solvers
    advection/      - Advection schemes (QUICK, ADBQUICKEST, etc.)
    fsi/            - Fluid-structure interaction
    multibody/      - Multi-rigid-body dynamics
    textbooks/      - Textbook chapters

After adding papers, re-index:
